About PrintLoad

iOS · Wireless Printing & Scanning

PrintLoad helps you scan documents, create PDFs, and print wirelessly from iPhone and iPad — fast, simple, and private.

Features

  • Document & ID scanning
  • Image to PDF and PDF tools
  • Print photos, files, and web pages
  • Local-first privacy — files stay on your device

PrintLoad (iOS) · Last updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PrintLoad (“the app”) handles information when you use our iOS application published on the App Store.

PrintPal does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email, or phone number for normal use of the app.

Your documents remain on your device. We do not upload them to our servers. We do not sell your personal data.
